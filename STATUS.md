## Headline (details in README)

Shrinkage mean beats every learned model on capped VW factors; Clark–West positive
(trees 3.66–4.25) but forecasts 2.5–5× too large; DM-trained ≈ EM-trained directionally;
no learned timing portfolio beats static net of 20 bp; the shrinkage-mean tilt is best in
every specification. Equal-weighted factors are the exception (learned models beat the
shrinkage mean and static; not the tilt).
